Age Groups

Junior Program: Ages 6-11

  • Focused on coordination, discipline, and basic techniques
  • Age-appropriate self-defense skills
  • Fun activities to build confidence

Senior Program: Ages 12+

  • Comprehensive Kenpo curriculum
  • Practical self-defense techniques
  • Belt advancement opportunities

What to Bring

For Your First Class:

  • Comfortable sports clothes (t-shirt and tracksuit bottoms)
  • Water bottle
  • Towel
  • Training shoes are optional (we train barefoot)

After Joining:

  • Kenpo uniform (gi) - available for purchase at the dojo
  • Belt (provided with uniform purchase)

We recommend removing all jewelry before class

Pricing

Membership Fees:

  • Junior (Under 12): €5 per class
  • Senior (12+): €7 per class
  • Adult (16+): €10 per class
  • Family Discount: 10% off for each additional family member

One-time Costs:

  • Registration Fee: €40 (includes insurance)
  • Uniform (Gi): €60 - €80 depending on size

Trial Class: First class is FREE!

Payment methods: Cash per class or bank transfer per month
