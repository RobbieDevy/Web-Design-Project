About Us

Welcome to Three Rock Kenpo Karate!

We are a martial arts school located in Dublin, Ireland, dedicated to teaching the art and discipline of American Kenpo Karate. Our club offers a friendly, inclusive environment where students of all ages and experience levels can learn effective self-defence, improve fitness, and build confidence.

At Three Rock Kenpo, we focus on practical techniques, personal development, and respect—both on and off the mats. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to progress through the ranks, our experienced instructors are here to guide and support you on your martial arts journey.

Join us and discover the strength, skill, and community that make our club truly special.
