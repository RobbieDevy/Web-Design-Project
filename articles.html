Robbie - Student, Green belt

Robbie has been training in Kenpo Karate for 10 years and currently holds the rank of green belt. His dedication and consistency over the years reflect a deep passion for martial arts and a genuine love for the journey.

Through his time at Three Rock Kenpo, Robbie has developed strong fundamentals, improved his fitness, and gained confidence both on and off the mats. He's known for his positive attitude, steady progress, and commitment to continuous learning.

Robbie is a valued member of the club, always bringing focus and enthusiasm to every class as he works toward his next rank.

Amy Bryne - Instructor, Black belt

Amy has been practicing Kenpo Karate for over 20 years and holds the rank of 3rd degree black belt. Her dedication, depth of knowledge, and passion for teaching make her a cornerstone of Three Rock Kenpo.

Known for her precise technique and calm, focused teaching style, Amy brings a wealth of experience to every class. She has a natural ability to break down complex movements and help students of all levels understand both the physical and mental aspects of Kenpo.

Whether she's teaching a beginner's class or fine-tuning techniques with advanced students, Amy leads by example, and is a well-respected member of the club.

Finn Ryan - Instructor, Black belt

Finn is a 6th degree black belt with over 35 years of experience in Kenpo Karate. He began training as a teenager and has since dedicated his life to mastering the art and sharing it with others. His depth of knowledge, sharp technique, and leadership make him one of the most respected figures in the Three Rock Kenpo community.

As a senior instructor, Finn brings a wealth of experience to the dojo. He's known for his high standards, insightful instruction, and ability to bring out the best in his students. His teaching blends tradition with practical application, always with a focus on discipline, respect, and personal growth.

Finn continues to inspire students of all levels, serving not only as a teacher, but as a living example of lifelong commitment to martial arts.
