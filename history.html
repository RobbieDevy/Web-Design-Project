THE HISTORY OF KENPO KARATE

From Ancient Origins to Modern Practice

The Kenpo Journey

American Kenpo Karate has evolved through centuries of martial arts development, blending traditional techniques with modern innovations. Follow the timeline below to discover the rich history and heritage of the art we practice today.

Ancient Origins

5th Century

Shaolin Beginnings

The roots of Kenpo can be traced back to the Shaolin Temple in China, where monks developed fighting systems for self-defense. These early martial arts combined physical techniques with philosophical principles that would later influence Kenpo's development.

Journey to Okinawa

17th Century

The Birth of Karate

Chinese martial arts migrated to Okinawa, where they merged with local fighting styles to create Te (Hand), the precursor to modern karate. These arts were practiced in secret due to weapons bans imposed by ruling Japanese authorities.

Modern Kenpo Emerges

Early 1900s

James Mitose & William Chow

James Mitose introduced Kenpo to Hawaii, teaching what he called "Kenpo Jiu-Jitsu." His student William Chow later modified these teachings to create a more street-effective style that would become known as Chinese Kempo of Hawaii.

American Kenpo

1950s-1960s

Ed Parker's Innovations

Ed Parker, a student of William Chow, brought Kenpo to the mainland United States and revolutionized the art. He systematized the curriculum, added modern scientific principles, and created what we now know as American Kenpo Karate. Parker's system emphasized practical self-defense and logical movement principles.

Global Expansion

1970s-Present

Worldwide Recognition

After Ed Parker's passing in 1990, his senior students continued to spread American Kenpo worldwide. The art has continued to evolve with various organizations preserving Parker's teachings while adapting to modern self-defense needs.

Three Rock Kenpo's Heritage

Three Rock Kenpo was established in Dublin, Ireland in 2009 by Finn Ryan, who is committed to preserving and sharing the art of American Kenpo Karate. Finn has have trained under respected lineage holders who can trace their training directly back to Ed Parker.

Over the years, we've grown from a handful of students to a thriving community of martial artists of all ages and backgrounds. We honor the rich traditions of Kenpo while embracing modern teaching methods to ensure our students receive the most effective training possible.
